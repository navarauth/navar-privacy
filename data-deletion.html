Delete your NavAR data

How to ask us to delete your account and the data we keep about you.

The short version: fill in our request form (or send us an email) and we will delete your NavAR account and personal data within 30 days.

How to delete your data

  1. Open the NavAR data request form with the button below.
  2. Type the email you used to sign up for NavAR. If you signed up with Facebook, use the email on your Facebook account.
  3. Type your full name as it appears in your NavAR account.
  4. Choose Delete my account and personal data.
  5. Tick the box that says you own the account, then submit the form.

Open the data request form

Prefer email?

Send an email to jennymarie.itaas@gmail.com with the subject Delete my NavAR account. Send it from the email address you used to sign up, so we can match it to your account.

What we delete

  • Your NavAR account and login details
  • Your name and email address
  • Profile details you gave us, such as your date of birth
  • The name, email, and profile picture we received if you logged in with Facebook
  • Your activity linked to your account, such as the promotions you viewed or clicked

What happens next

  1. We may reply to check that the request really comes from you. This keeps other people from deleting your account.
  2. We delete your account and personal data within 30 days.
  3. We send you an email to confirm it is done.

Copies of your data in backups and system logs are removed within one month after your account is deleted.

We may keep combined, anonymous numbers, such as the total clicks on a promotion. They cannot be used to identify you.

If you signed up with Facebook

Sending us a deletion request removes your data from NavAR. You can also remove NavAR from your Facebook account so that Facebook stops sharing information with it:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings.
  2. Find Apps and Websites. Facebook may also call this Business Integrations.
  3. Select NavAR and choose Remove.

Removing NavAR in Facebook does not delete the data we already hold, so please send us the deletion request as well.
